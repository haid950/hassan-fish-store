Koi care guide

Happy koi, healthy pond

Koi can live for decades. These basics cover most of what keeps them thriving.

01

Acclimatise slowly

Float the sealed bag in your pond for 20–30 minutes so the temperatures match. Then add a little pond water every 5 minutes before releasing your koi.

02

Give them space

Plan for around 1,000 litres for your first koi and 500 litres for each extra one. Aim for at least 1 metre of depth to protect them from heat and predators.

03

Filter and aerate

Turn over the pond's full volume through a biological filter every 1–2 hours. Run an air pump all the time, especially in summer when warm water holds less oxygen.

04

Feed little and often

Feed only what they finish in 3–5 minutes, 2–3 times a day. Cut back below 15°C and stop feeding below 10°C, when their digestion slows right down.

05

Beat the summer heat

Pakistani summers can push ponds past 32°C. Shade at least a third of the surface, top up with dechlorinated water, and increase aeration.

06

Watch for warning signs

Clamped fins, gasping at the surface, flashing against walls or red streaks can all signal a problem. Test the water first, then call us. We're happy to help.
